# Optional Demo Video Script

Chrome Web Store video is optional and must be added as a YouTube URL in the Dashboard. Use this script if you decide to create one.

Target length: 35-45 seconds.

## Shot List

1. Open a new tab and show Del or Keep presenting one old bookmark.
2. Click Open to show that the bookmark opens normally in a new tab.
3. Return to the Del or Keep tab and click Keep to remove the item from the queue.
4. Open another new tab and click Delete.
5. Show the confirmation step.
6. Confirm delete and show the undo toast.
7. Click undo to restore the bookmark.
8. End on the queue summary panel.

## Voiceover

Del or Keep helps you clean up old Chrome bookmarks one new tab at a time. It only reviews bookmarks saved at least 30 days ago. Open before deciding, keep what still matters, or delete with confirmation and undo. Review state stays local in Chrome, with no account, analytics, ads, or external network permissions.
